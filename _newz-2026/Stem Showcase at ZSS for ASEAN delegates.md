<p>Zhenghua Secondary School recently welcomed ASEAN delegates for a visit
centred on our Applied Learning Programme (ALP). During the visit, students
shared how authentic STEM experiences — spanning design thinking, prototyping,
and drone activities — have shaped their learning journey.</p>
<p>Through these hands-on experiences, students develop key competencies
including communication, adaptive thinking, and inventive thinking, nurturing
them into confident learners. The delegates saw how the ALP empowers students
to design inclusive solutions, preparing them to contribute meaningfully
to future-ready communities.</p>
