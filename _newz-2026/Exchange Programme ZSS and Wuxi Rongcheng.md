<p>The two-day exchange at Zhenghua brought together students from Wuxi Rongcheng
Experimental Middle School and our school community for a meaningful journey
of cultural learning. Having visited Rongcheng the previous year, our Secondary
3 students were especially glad to welcome their friends back on home ground.
Highlights of the visit included joint performances, discussions on Racial
Harmony, and local exploration, forging lasting friendships through shared
lessons and immersive cultural experiences.
<br>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Newz 2026/Exchange Programme/exchange_program_4.jpg">
</div>
<p>My Rongcheng buddy translated the guide’s explanation of Peranakan culture
as we explored Joo Chiat. Despite the language barrier, we had a great
time discussing our schools. While I wish I had communicated better and
shared more about Singapore’s culture, I’m glad we connected through photos
and shared memories.
<br>Alejandro Gwen Corpuz (3A)</p>
<p>I was surprised by how naturally the Rongcheng students and I conversed
despite the language barrier and having never met before. They were incredibly
easygoing and eager to learn about Singapore. I also really enjoyed taking
photos with them.
<br>Mildred Eve Lim Han (3C)</p>
<p>I'll fondly remember our attempts to speak each other's languages, taking
photos in ethnic costumes, and our funny moments together. I found explaining
complex cultural topics in Chinese challenging, but I've learned I should
be less hesitant to strike up conversations in the future.
<br>Shervelle Tan Qian Ning, (3G)</p>
