<h2>Outdoor Education: Character &amp; Leadership Development</h2>
<p>
<br>At Zhenghua Secondary School, our Learning for Life Programme (LLP) in
Outdoor Education provides students with authentic learning experiences
beyond the classroom. Through progressively challenging outdoor activities,
students develop resilience, leadership, teamwork and environmental responsibility
while building confidence and a spirit of adventure.&nbsp;</p>
<hr>
<h3>Bringing Our Vision and Mission to Life</h3>
<p>Each outdoor learning experience is designed to nurture character, inspire
excellence, and develop Principled Leaders, Passionate Learners and Gracious
Citizens.
<br>
</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Principled Leadership Through Ownership<br></strong> Outdoor experiences
encourage students to take ownership by planning routes, making safety
decisions and leading their peers with empathy, integrity and sound judgement.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Passionate Learning Through Practical Mastery<br></strong> Outdoor
education equips students with practical skills in navigation, trip planning,
outdoor cooking and shelter building, developing confidence and problem-solving
abilities.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Gracious Citizenship and Environmental Stewardship<br></strong> Secondary
1 Inter-Disciplinary Trails immerse students in Singapore's historically
significant blue and green spaces, fostering a sense of place, appreciation
of local heritage and environmental responsibility.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>

<h3>Embodying the Zhenghua Creed and Values</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Resilience and Courage:</strong> Stepping beyond comfort zones
and persevering through challenges.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Teamwork and Trust:</strong> Working together through communication,
collaboration and mutual support.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Responsibility and Care:</strong> Demonstrating responsibility
through outdoor ethics, risk awareness and respect for others and the environment.</p>
</li>
</ul>
